About

Hi, I’m YongKang.

I’m an electrical and computer engineering student at Olin College, using statistics and computational mathematics to understand and optimise complex real-world systems.

I learn things by rebuilding them. My Naive Bayes classifier uses no ML libraries; my Monopoly agent trains inside an engine I wrote in the standard library; my cart-pole agent learns in physics I implemented myself. Reaching for the library comes after — once I know what it is doing for me.

The other half of that habit is measurement. Most of what I know about modelling I learned from something that turned out to be wrong: a leaky split, a reward that optimised the wrong thing, a “+2%” that was noise. So I write up what didn’t work as carefully as what did — every repo here has a README a stranger can run.

What I’m looking for: a Summer 2027 internship in machine learning, reinforcement learning or applied research — somewhere models reach production and evaluation is taken seriously.
